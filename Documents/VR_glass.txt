Device: VR Glasses (Virtual Reality Headsets)
Category: Digital wellbeing and reminiscence support

Description:
VR glasses are an immersive visual and auditory technology designed to project interactive or 360-degree virtual environments to support cognitive, emotional, and social wellbeing.

Main functions:

Displays familiar historical places, natural landscapes, or environments from a person's past.

Provides calming virtual experiences such as forests, beaches, mountains, or peaceful gardens.

Offers interactive games and activities designed for mental stimulation.

Delivers guided breathing and relaxation environments.

Enables virtual travel to distant countries, cities, and museums.

Suitable situations:

Elderly individuals who experience loneliness, boredom, or a repetitive daily routine.

People with memory difficulties or cognitive decline (such as dementia).

Older adults with limited physical mobility who can no longer travel easily.

Individuals needing stress reduction, relaxation, or mood elevation.

Benefits:
The device can stimulate memories,  encourage reminiscence and social conversation, reduce anxiety, promote gentle physical movement, and improve overall quality of life.
it helps on your mood and better the feeling if you feel lonely.
it will entertain you and you can watch your shows and movies.

Important:
The information in this document is for general information. A healthcare professional or caregiver should assess whether VR experiences are suitable and comfortable for an individual person.
