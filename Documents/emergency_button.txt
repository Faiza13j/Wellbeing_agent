Device: Emergency Button (Personal Emergency Response System / SOS Alarm)
Category: Immediate call and safety support

Description:
An Emergency Button is a simple, wearable or stationary alarm device designed to provide instant communication with emergency services, care providers, or family members during sudden health crises or accidents.

Main functions:

Triggers an immediate emergency alert with a single press of a button.

Connects directly to a 24/7 emergency response center, home care team, or family contact.

Features integrated fall detection (on select wearable models) to trigger alerts automatically if the user cannot press the button.

Operates continuously in or around the home (via wristband, pendant, or wall unit) to minimize response times.

Suitable situations:

Older adults living alone who are at risk of falls, sudden illness, or physical accidents.

Individuals with mobility impairments or balance issues.

Seniors recovering from medical procedures or managing chronic health conditions.

Families seeking immediate notification and reassurance in case of an emergency at home.

Benefits:
The device drastically reduces the time needed to receive emergency medical help, mitigates the dangers of prolonged post-fall lying, boosts living confidence, and enables safer long-term independent living.

Important:
Regular testing, battery checks, and clear emergency contact protocols are essential. Family members or care managers should ensure the user is comfortable wearing or accessing the button at all times.
