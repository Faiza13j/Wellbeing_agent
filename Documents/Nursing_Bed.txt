Nursing Bed

A nursing bed is an electrically adjustable bed designed to support people who need assistance with sleeping, resting, changing position, or getting in and out of bed.

Nursing beds are commonly used in care homes, hospitals, assisted living environments, and sometimes at home.

Main features may include:

* Electrically adjustable backrest
* Adjustable leg or foot section
* Height adjustment
* Adjustable bed position to support comfortable sitting or lying
* Side rails or other safety features, depending on the model
* Hand control for adjusting the bed
* Wheels and brakes to make moving and positioning the bed easier

Benefits of a nursing bed include:

* Improves comfort and positioning
* Makes it easier for a person to get in and out of bed
* Helps caregivers provide safer care
* Makes activities such as washing, dressing, and repositioning easier
* Allows the bed height to be adjusted for different care situations
* Can support safer transfers between the bed and other equipment

A nursing bed should be adjusted according to the user's needs and the instructions provided by the manufacturer. Caregivers should make sure that safety features such as brakes and side rails are used correctly.

The exact functions and safety features depend on the nursing bed model. Users and caregivers should follow the manufacturer's instructions and the care organization's safety guidelines.
