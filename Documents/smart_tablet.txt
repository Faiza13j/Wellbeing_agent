Device: Smart Tablet (Senior Tablet)
Category: Digital care and daily communication support

Description:
A Smart Tablet for seniors is a simple touchscreen device loaded with accessible software that helps older adults maintain daily routines, communicate with care networks, and access digital activities.

Main functions:

Provides automated reminders for wake-up times, medications, showering, meals, hydration, and appointments.

Displays a clear daily schedule of planned routines and activities.

Facilitates direct video calling and messaging with family members, friends, and care staff (hoitaja).

Streams live or recorded online wellness classes, including exercise (Jumppa), music, and language courses.

Offers interactive brain-training activities, memory exercises, and casual games.

Enables remote care monitoring so caregivers can track whether essential routines are being followed.

Suitable situations:

Older adults living independently who need daily support with scheduling and medication routines.

Seniors with mild memory challenges who benefit from visual and auditory prompts.

Individuals experiencing isolation or limited physical mobility who want to stay connected with family and health professionals.

Elderly individuals looking for simple, accessible mental stimulation and home-based exercise.

Benefits:
The tablet reduces loneliness through direct social connection, supports daily routine compliance, promotes cognitive health, and helps older adults live safely and independently at home.

Important:
The information in this document is for general information. Individual needs and tech-literacy levels should be assessed by family members or healthcare providers to choose the appropriate tablet interface and settings.
