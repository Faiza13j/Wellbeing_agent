Smart Tags

Smart Tags are small tracking or identification devices that can help users find and keep track of important items. They can be attached to **glasses, home keys, mobile phones, bags, or other personal belongings**.

Possible benefits:

* Helps find lost or misplaced items
* Supports independence and everyday safety
* Can connect to a smartphone or other device, depending on the model
* Useful for older adults or people who may have difficulty remembering where they placed their belongings

The exact features depend on the type of Smart Tag and the technology it uses.
