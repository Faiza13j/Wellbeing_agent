Device: Safety Band

Category: Personal safety

Description:
A safety band is a wearable wellbeing technology device
that can support safety and emergency assistance.

Main functions:
- Can be worn on the wrist.
- Can provide a way to request help.
- Can support safety at home and outside the home.

Suitable situations:
- Elderly people living independently.
- People who may need emergency assistance.
- People who are concerned about personal safety.

Benefits:
The device can provide additional support and reassurance
for independent living.

Important:
The exact functions depend on the specific safety band model.
