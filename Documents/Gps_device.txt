Device: GPS Tracking Device (Personal Safety & Location Tracker)
Category: Mobile safety and orientation support

Description:
A GPS Tracking Device is a wearable or portable location-monitoring tool that provides real-time geographic tracking and emergency communication to support safe outdoor mobility.

Main functions:

Provides real-time geographic location tracking via companion mobile apps or caregiver portals.

Features geofencing to send instant alerts if the user strays outside designated safe areas.

Includes a one-touch SOS emergency button for immediate assistance.

Delivers location updates when the user moves outside the home, ensuring continuous safety coverage.

Suitable situations:

Older adults living independently who enjoy walking or traveling outdoors alone.

Individuals with cognitive decline, dementia, or memory loss who are prone to getting lost or confused.

Seniors at risk of wandering or spatial disorientation.

Families and caregivers seeking continuous peace of mind regarding a loved one's location.

Benefits:
The device promotes physical activity and outdoor independence while drastically reducing the risk of missing-person incidents, enabling quick emergency responses, and providing reassurance to caregivers.

Important:
Proper setup, battery management, and user consent are essential. Family members or care teams should configure safe zones and alert preferences based on the individual's routines and cognitive needs.
