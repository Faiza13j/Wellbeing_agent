Device:Axitare Medication Dispenser
Category: Medication support

Description:
Axitare is an automated medication dispensing system
designed to support people with their medication routines.

Main functions:
- Reminds the user when medication should be taken.
- Dispenses the medication at the correct time.
- Helps organize medication doses.
- Can support independent living.

Suitable situations:
- People who sometimes forget to take medication.
- Elderly people who need support with medication routines.
- People who need assistance managing regular medication.

Benefits:
The device can help make medication routines easier and
more organized.

Important:
The information in this document is for general information.
A healthcare professional should assess whether the device
is suitable for an individual person.
