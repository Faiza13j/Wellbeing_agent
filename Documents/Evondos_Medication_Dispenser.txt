DEVICE: Evondos Medication Dispenser

CATEGORY: Medication support

DESCRIPTION:
Evondos is an automated medication dispensing system
designed to support medication routines.

FUNCTIONS:
- Provides medication reminders.
- Dispenses medication at scheduled times.
- Helps organize medication doses.
- Can support independent living.

SUITABLE FOR:
- People who need support with medication routines.
- People who sometimes forget to take medication.
- People who need assistance with regular medication.

BENEFITS:
- Supports regular medication routines.
- Can reduce the need to remember medication times.

IMPORTANT:
This document provides general information.
Suitability for an individual should be assessed by
an appropriate healthcare professional.
