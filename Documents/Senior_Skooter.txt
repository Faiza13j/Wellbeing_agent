The scooter is designed for the elderly and people with limited mobility who are looking for an independent way to get around and increase their freedom of movement comfortably and easily.
The driving range is 30 km, the maximum speed is 6.5 km/h and the seat height is adjustable between 42–55 cm.
Features and benefits:
- Easy battery installation: The batteries are installed in their own places without wires or other connections and are ready to use immediately.
- Stepless handlebar adjustment: The handlebar is released by turning the lever to the right and locked when you release the lever. In addition, the handlebar can be folded all the way down, keeping the items inside the basket safely in place.
- Easy to transport: The scooter can be disassembled into two parts simply and quickly, in just three steps, so you can easily take it with you.
- Excellent legroom: Provides comfort and space for your legs.
