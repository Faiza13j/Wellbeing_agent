Accu-Chek SmartGuide Sensor – Continuous Glucose Monitoring with Predictive Technology
The Accu-Chek SmartGuide sensor is an innovative CGM (continuous glucose monitoring) sensor that provides real-time information about 
your blood glucose levels without the need for fingersticks or scanning. This smart glucose sensor continuously measures tissue glucose 
levels and automatically sends results to your smartphone via Bluetooth every five minutes. This way, you always have up-to-date 
information about your glucose control, wherever you are.

Can I use an Accu-Chek sensor with the app?
The SmartGuide and Predict apps provide a complete overview of glucose values, trend arrows, glucose changes, insulin doses, 
carbohydrate intake and physical activity. Advanced algorithms automatically recognize glucose curves, supporting treatment assessment 
and behavioral adjustments. This makes the sensor particularly suitable for adults with diabetes who are striving for optimal glycemic 
control.

A key benefit of the Accu-Chek SmartGuide continuous glucose monitor sensor is its predictive functionality. The Predict app warns of 
an increased risk of hypoglycemia within 30 minutes and provides an overall glucose forecast up to 2 hours ahead. It also offers 7 hours 
of overnight hypoglycemia prediction, providing additional reassurance during sleep and helping to reduce the risk of nocturnal 
hypoglycemia.

The sensor is comfortable, discreet and water resistant up to 1 meter for up to 60 minutes. Up to 14 days of battery life and automatic 
sensor replacement reminder ensure ease of use. Continuous glucose monitoring works seamlessly with iOS, Android and Apple Watch.

With the Accu-Chek SmartGuide sensor, you choose reliable and modern diabetes technology that combines real-time monitoring with 
predictive data, allowing you to take proactive measures and stay within your target glucose range. The package includes one sensor
 and is backed by a warranty.
